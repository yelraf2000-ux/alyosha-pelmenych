3D animated feature film shot, cinematic, night on a snowy slope of Mount Ararat. The man in the peach cap walks through the snow. Suddenly a small glowing golden dumpling (a round pelmen with pinched edges) streaks down from the starry sky like a shooting star and lands softly in the snow in front of him on the right, with a puff of snow. It keeps glowing warm golden light and gently steams. The man stops, eyes wide with astonishment, and slowly leans toward it, his face lit by the warm glow. Slow cinematic push-in camera move. Sound: quiet wind, a magical falling whistle, a soft thud in snow, a gentle warm hum. No dialogue, no speech, no narration, no on-screen text.
